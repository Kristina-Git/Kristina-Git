# Castle Rescue

A kid-friendly fighting game made for Lian (age 5). The Silly Knights locked Queen Mama and
Queen Kristina in the tallest tower, and Lian and Uncle Gosel battle to see who gets to save them.
The winner rescues the queens. The loser gets turned into a frog.

Open `index.html` in any web browser to play. It's a single file with no build step and nothing to install.

- **Battle for the Queens!**: Lian vs Uncle Gosel, best of three rounds across the Gate Hall,
  Banquet Hall and Throne Room, with Silly Knights getting in everyone's way. Play against the
  computer or with two players on one keyboard. The winner frees the queens, and Queen Mama's
  wand turns the loser into a frog (it wears off at bedtime).
- **Team Up vs the Knights** (bonus story): Lian and Uncle Gosel work together through three rooms
  and a boss, Sir Clank-a-Lot. One player gets a computer helper.
- **Lian's drawings**: on the title screen, add photos of Lian's drawings and they hang in gold
  frames on the castle walls. They're saved in that browser only.

Nobody gets hurt. Bonked knights get dizzy and run away, and a hero who runs out of hearts takes a short nap.

| | Walk | Jump | Bonk | Super power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One player | Arrows or A/D | Up or W | Space or F | G |
| Two players: Lian | A/D | W | F | G |
| Two players: Uncle Gosel | Left/Right arrows | Up arrow | Enter | Right Shift |

On tablets, round buttons appear on the screen. Press P to pause.
