# Castle Rescue

A kid-friendly fighting game made for Lian (age 5). Brave Lian and Uncle Gosel fight their way
through the castle to rescue Queen Mama and Queen Kristina from the Silly Knights.

Open `index.html` in any web browser to play. It's a single file with no build step and nothing to install.

- **Save the Queens!**: three rooms (Gate Hall, Banquet Hall, Throne Room boss), then the tower finale.
  One player gets a computer helper. Two players can share a keyboard.
- **Pillow Duel**: Lian vs Uncle Gosel, best of three rounds, against the computer or a second player.
- **Lian's drawings**: on the title screen, add photos of Lian's drawings and they hang in gold
  frames on the castle walls. They're saved in that browser only.

Nobody gets hurt. Bonked knights get dizzy and run away, and a hero who runs out of hearts takes a short nap.

| | Walk | Jump | Bonk | Super power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One player | Arrows or A/D | Up or W | Space or F | G |
| Two players: Lian | A/D | W | F | G |
| Two players: Uncle Gosel | Left/Right arrows | Up arrow | Enter | Right Shift |

On tablets, round buttons appear on the screen. Press P to pause.
